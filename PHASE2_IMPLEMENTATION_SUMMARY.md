# Phase 2 Implementation Summary

## What changed

Phase 2 adds the operational core identified in the comprehensive review. The
resource now gives beginners more practice connecting indications, limits,
protection actions, communications, and operating plans to real-time decisions.

### Nine new lesson sections

1. Human performance in the control room
2. SCADA, EMS, and data quality
3. Voltage-control equipment and generator limits
4. From Facility Ratings to real-time action
5. Protection schemes and event reconstruction
6. Operating Plans, EEA, and degraded tools
7. Restoration plans and electrical constraints
8. Directives, failed channels, and data verification
9. Interchange lifecycle and disturbance recovery

### Seven new labs

- Bad-data and topology diagnosis
- Voltage-control dispatch
- SOL/IROL corrective-action planning
- Protection sequence-of-events reconstruction
- Emergency operations tabletop
- Three-part communications and inability-to-comply practice
- Interchange lifecycle and schedule-ramp application
